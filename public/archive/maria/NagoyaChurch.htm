The Orthodox Church in Nagoya
The Holy Annunciation Church
Pascha

The church in Nagoya is a small parish church where 40 to 50 people join the Liturgy on Sunday. On Pascha 120-150 people, including people from various countries, share the joy.

The Paschal Greeting was exchanged in Japanese, Greek, Slavonic, English, Georgian, Albanian, Arabic, Romanian, etc.

The Gospel was read aloud in various languages by the delegates of those countries. Our church is small but seems to be linked throughout the world.

On Sundays most of attendants join singing. Even non-Japanese people participate, using partly Japanese texts written in English letter.
